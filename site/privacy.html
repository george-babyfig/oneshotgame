English

Operator: OWNER NAME (placeholder). Contact: support@YOUR-DOMAIN (placeholder). Comet Garden has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. We do not collect data from the app. Progress, settings, purchases recorded in the app and a small play ledger stay on the device; they may be included in an iCloud or computer device backup. Apple processes purchases; we do not see payment details. Optional reminders remain on the device.

Local data stays until you remove it. Use Settings → Reset progress to erase progress, Grown-ups → Clear play history to erase the play ledger, or delete the app to remove its local data. A device backup may retain a copy under your backup settings. We cannot delete an Apple-managed backup for you.

Game Center is Apple's optional service, used here for achievements only. Sign-in and its dashboard require the parent check; you can turn Game Center off. Apple handles Game Center data under its own privacy terms.

Sharing is optional and starts only after the parent check. A shared planet picture can show the planet, its title, subtitle and stars. A Passport picture shows the explorer name and number, title, portrait, chapter/star/creature counts and chosen badges. Other optional pictures can show a Homeworld, scrapbook page or expedition postcard, including artwork, creature or explorer name chosen in the game. Shared challenge text contains an alphanumeric planet seed, score and check character. The iOS share sheet sends only to the destination you choose; review the picture or text before sharing.

A tester build may copy a short Balance Report code with an adult's consent; nothing is sent automatically. If you email support, we use the email and any details you send only to answer, then delete the message. If a message appears to come from a child, we reply once asking for a grown-up and delete it. The website host may keep standard access logs; the operator must confirm its retention when hosting is chosen. For refunds and full purchase history, contact Apple.

Compartilhar é opcional e exige controle parental. Uma imagem de planeta pode mostrar o planeta, título, subtítulo e estrelas. A imagem do Passaporte mostra nome e número do explorador, título, retrato, quantidades de capítulos, estrelas e criaturas e emblemas escolhidos. 共有は任意で、保護者チェックの後だけ行えます。惑星の画像には惑星、タイトル、サブタイトル、星が含まれる場合があります。パスポート画像には探検家の名前と番号、称号、肖像、チャプター・星・いきものの数、選んだバッジが表示されます。ほかの画像にはホームワールド、アルバムのページ、探検の絵はがきや、ゲーム内で選んだいきもの・探検家の名前が含まれる場合があります。チャレンジの文には英数字の惑星シード、スコア、確認文字が入ります。iOSの共有先を選ぶ前に内容を確認してください。
