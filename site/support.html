How do I restore purchases?

  1. Open Comet Garden and tap the gear to open Settings.
  2. Tap Grown-ups and answer the parent check.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

Make sure the iPhone is signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. One-time purchases come back this way. Gems, including gems from gem packs, live in your progress on this device, so they can't be restored.

How does Ask to Buy work?

Ask to Buy is part of Apple's Family Sharing. When it's on, a purchase request goes to the family organizer, who can approve or decline it on their own device. Nothing is charged unless it's approved.

If a request is approved later, the purchase is delivered the next time Comet Garden is open. The family organizer turns Ask to Buy on in Family Sharing settings. Screen Time can also limit purchases.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on this device. Comet Garden has no account or game server, so we don't have a copy. Local UserDefaults may be included in your device backup through iCloud or a computer, depending on your backup settings.

Deleting the app removes its local progress; a prior device backup may still contain a copy. We can't restore it for you. Reset progress in Settings erases it too. Purchases made with your Apple Account can still be restored (see Restore purchases).

How do I turn on Gentle planets?

  1. Tap the gear to open Settings.
  2. Tap Grown-ups and answer the parent check.
  3. Under Grown-up settings, turn on Gentle planets.

With Gentle planets on, sky bumps give the throw back, and Troubles and Clashes rest. Stars count as normal.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles every purchase and refund. We never see payment details, so we can't issue refunds ourselves.

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  2. Choose I'd like to, then Request a refund.
  3. Pick a reason, choose the Comet Garden purchase and submit.

Apple reviews each request and lets you know. Your full purchase history is there too.

Does Comet Garden collect any data?

No. Comet Garden has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and progress stays on the device. Read the privacy policy for the details, in six languages.
