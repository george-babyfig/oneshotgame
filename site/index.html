Fling and grow

Every throw changes the world

Gravity bends each comet around the planet, and what it carries decides what grows.

  • Pull back, aim and let go: gravity bends every throw around the planet
  • Rocks raise mountains, ice comets fill oceans, seed pods grow meadows and forests, magma builds volcanoes, rain clouds and sunbursts help it all thrive
  • The planet shows what your throw will do before you let go: new land, who moves in, and anyone who might wander off

Meet the creatures

Cute friends move in

Build the right home and someone new comes to live there.

  • 36 creatures to find, each with a favourite home: otters where forest meets the sea, newts on warm volcanoes, owls in snowy pines
  • Every creature brings a trait that protects its home
  • Keep them all in your Lifebook, from common friends to rare legends

Mix, chain and plan

Clever throws, bigger magic

Put the right throws together and watch what they make.

  • Fusions: two throws that work together, like ice and magma making Steam
  • Combos: clever throws back to back grow a chain of bonus power
  • Supernova: charge it up and send a super-sized throw
  • Read the sky: drifting rocks, a bubble moon and a turning ring of pebbles to time your throws around

A Homeworld of your own

A home for every friend

The friends you meet can come and live with you.

  • Invite the friends you meet to live on your Homeworld
  • Choose a Buddy who helps when a Trouble appears on a planet
  • Make your Keeper: faces, skin tones, hair and outfits

Play your way

Gentle to start, lots to explore

More than 100 planets ramp up gently, with 1 to 3 stars on each. When a planet is tricky, a help ladder shows what happened, offers a tip, and then a little extra help.

  • Daily Planet
  • Weekly Voyage
  • Meteor Rush
  • Zen Garden
  • Friend challenges

For grown-ups

Calm, fair and private

The things grown-ups ask about first, answered up front.

  • No ads

    There are no ads anywhere in the game.

  • No energy timers

    No lives and no energy timers. Nobody waits for a meter to refill.

  • No random paid rewards

    Every optional purchase shows exactly what you get.

  • Purchases stay with grown-ups

    Optional purchases are sold only in the Grown-ups area, behind a parent check.

  • Gentle planets

    A setting in the Grown-ups area: sky bumps give the throw back, and Troubles and Clashes rest.

  • Play-time summary

    See this week's rounds and minutes, and set an optional break reminder.

Data Not Collected

No accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Progress stays on this device. Read the privacy policy.
