Dev notes

News and release notes from the Comet Garden team.

Version 1.0 · 2026

Hello, Comet Garden

Hi! Comet Garden 1.0 is our very first release, and we're so happy to share it.

Comet Garden is a small game about big throws. You pull back, aim and let go, and gravity bends your comet around a tiny planet. Rocks raise mountains, ice comets fill oceans, and seed pods grow meadows and forests. Then the creatures arrive: otters where forest meets the sea, newts on warm volcanoes, owls in snowy pines. Each one brings a trait that protects its home, and the friends you meet can come and live on your Homeworld.

What we care about

No ads. Nothing interrupts a planet. There are no lives or energy timers either, so nobody waits for a meter to refill.

Fair purchases, only for grown-ups. Optional purchases live in the Grown-ups area, behind a parent check. Each one shows exactly what you get, and there are no random paid rewards.

Gentle at the start, never a wall. Our rule for difficulty was simple: “not too hard at the beginning but also not too easy.” The first planets ramp up gently, and 1 to 3 stars on each give you something to aim for. When a planet is tricky, the help ladder steps in: what happened, a tip, and then a little extra help. Grown-ups can also turn on Gentle planets. Nobody should get stuck behind a wall, whatever their age.

Your data stays with you. No accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Progress stays on your device, and the App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected. The privacy policy has the details.

What's in 1.0

  • More than 100 planets, with 1 to 3 stars on each
  • Rocks, ice comets, seed pods, magma, rain clouds and sunbursts to throw
  • 36 creatures to find and keep in your Lifebook, from common friends to rare legends
  • Fusions, Combos and the Supernova throw
  • A sky to read: drifting rocks, a bubble moon and a turning ring of pebbles
  • Your own Homeworld, a Buddy who helps when a Trouble appears, and a Keeper to make your own
  • Daily Planet, Weekly Voyage, Meteor Rush, Zen Garden, friend challenges and Bonus Remix
  • Essences from planets and Labs on your Homeworld
  • English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português (Brasil) and 日本語
  • A Grown-ups area with Gentle planets, a play-time summary and an optional break reminder

What's next

We'd love to keep the garden growing: more planets and more friends to meet. We don't have dates to share, and we'd rather take our time and get things right.

Have an idea or found a bug? Tell us through the support page. We'd love to hear from you.

Happy flinging!

— the Comet Garden team
