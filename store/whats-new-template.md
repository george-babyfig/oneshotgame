# "What's New" template (from version 1.0.1)

Apple doesn't show "What's New" for a first release. From 1.0.1 on, keep it warm, specific and short: a headline, 3–5 concrete changes, and a sign-off. Name real changes (Guideline 2.3.12); no pressure words, prices or other games.

## Template

**<Headline: the one thing players will notice>**

• <New thing, in a sentence a child's parent understands>
• <Improvement>
• <Fix, said plainly ("Friends no longer hide behind the forecast strip")>

Thanks for playing, and happy flinging!
— the Comet Garden team

## Example (1.0.1)

**Smoother flings on smaller phones**

• The planet tag moves out of your finger's way even faster
• Clearer wall icons show which lands are safe from a Trouble
• Fixed a rare case where a Combo bead didn't light up

Thanks for playing, and happy flinging!
