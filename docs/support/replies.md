# Support replies — English source

Replace bracketed text only with verified facts. Use the sender's language after the support lead translates and reviews these replies. Do not ask for passwords, payment details, a child's name, or a full save file.

## Refund

Hi,

I'm sorry the purchase wasn't right for you. Apple handles Comet Garden payments and refunds. Please visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in with the Apple Account that made the purchase, choose “Request a refund,” and select the purchase. Apple will review the request and reply to you.

## Ask to Buy

Hi,

If your family uses Ask to Buy, Apple sends the purchase request to the family organizer, who can approve or decline it on their device. You can check Ask to Buy in iPhone Family Sharing settings. If a request was approved, reopen Comet Garden; if the item is still missing, go to Grown-ups and tap Restore Purchases. Please tell us what happened without sending payment details.

## Restore Purchases on a new phone or after reinstall

Hi,

On the iPhone, sign in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. In Comet Garden, open Settings → Grown-ups, pass the parent check, and tap Restore Purchases. Eligible one-time purchases can return. Gem packs and saved progress are local and cannot be restored by this button. A prior iPhone backup may contain progress, depending on your backup settings.

## Family Sharing

Hi,

Eligible Comet Garden looks may be shared with your Apple family if Family Sharing is set up for the Apple Accounts involved. On the receiving iPhone, open Settings → Grown-ups and tap Restore Purchases. Gem packs are consumable and are not shared. Apple manages Family Sharing eligibility and purchase records.

## Parental gate

Hi,

Grown-ups, purchases and sharing open after the parent check. In Settings → Grown-ups, answer the gate to open those controls. A parent PIN can be set there for later visits. If the gate is not working, please tell us the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and what you see; please do not send the PIN.

## My child lost progress

Hi,

I'm sorry the garden is missing. Comet Garden saves progress on the iPhone and has no account or server copy for us to restore. If this happened after moving phones or reinstalling, check whether an iCloud or computer device backup from before the loss is available. Restoring a device backup is managed through Apple and can replace newer device data, so review its date first. Purchased eligible looks can be restored separately from Grown-ups → Restore Purchases; gem packs and game progress cannot be restored that way.

## Deleting data

Hi,

Comet Garden keeps progress and settings on the iPhone. Settings → Reset progress clears game progress; Grown-ups → Clear play history clears the local play ledger. A short log of error types for the Diagnostic code also stays on the device. Deleting the app removes all this local data. A device backup may still hold a copy under your backup settings; Apple manages those backups. We do not have a game account or server copy to delete. If you emailed us, we use your message only to reply and then delete it.

## Bug report with a diagnostic code

Hi,

Thank you for reporting this. The diagnostic code you chose to send helps us compare broad error types and the app version; it contains no message text or personal details. Could a grown-up tell us what happened, which screen was open, and whether it happens again? The iPhone model and iOS version would also help. Please do not send a child's name, passwords, or payment details. We will look into it and reply when we know more.
