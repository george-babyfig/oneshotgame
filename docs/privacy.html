Pocket Planet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

English

Data Not Collected. Pocket Planet has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Progress and a small play ledger stay on this device. The Grown-ups area shows play time, purchases recorded here and settings. Apple processes purchases; we never see payment details. Optional reminders stay on the device. A tester build can copy a short Balance Report code only after an adult agrees; the code is not sent automatically. Shared challenge codes contain a planet code and score. For refunds and full purchase history, use Apple. Questions: contact the developer through the App Store listing.
