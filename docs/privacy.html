Pocket Planet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Pocket Planet does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Optional reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Challenge codes you share contain only a planet code and a score.

Questions? Contact the developer through the App Store listing.
