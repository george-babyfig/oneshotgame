## 0. Read this first

### 0.1 Seven facts that change the plan

1. **The frost band is already broken at HEAD, and no gate checks it.** `tests/sim/reactions.sim.ts` measures `frostPerTen = 19.8` (§0.2) against the RM §7.5 band of 3-10 (RM:835). The career sim agrees: Regular earns about 16.6 frost per 10 first clears by day 14. So "frost stays at 3-10 per 10 planets" can't be a literal M13 gate. Decision 57 re-baselines it.
2. **The sim's Regular player has no Launch Bay until day 47-48, so no trip gate can be measured today.**
   - `report.json` gives Regular `homeLevelDays {2:4, 3:8, 4:19, 5:47}` and its first trip payout on day 48. Engaged's first trip is on day 29.
   - Why: Labs, the Den, the Greenhouse and decorations fill every plot through HW L3. From HW L4, the sim saves for Level 5 and builds nothing (`savingForHome`, career.ts:412).
   - Even with an early Bay, Bay 3 (4-hour trips) needs HW L3 (day 8) plus 7,600 ✨ of Bay builds. Regular's Labs are short of stardust on days 8-11 and 19-25.
   - So decision 56 first fixes the sim's Bay order on the 1.0 code (step 0), then measures the day Regular's Bay reaches level 3 ("Bay-3 day"). The 30% window starts there. If that day is after day 8, RM's "by day 14" becomes a Watch.
   - Until then, any "trips' share" gate passes or fails on zero trips. This includes the share for days 8-14 and the cumulative share for days 1-14.
3. **Today's trips are the opposite of the spec.**
   - **Today:** you pick the friend and the planet is random. Trips pay stardust, gems and boosters, give no friendship, aren't sped up by wins, and open at Bay level 1/2/3.
   - **The spec:** you pick the planet and the game picks the friend. Trips pay Essences plus up to 3 gems, give +1 to +3 friendship, lose 10 minutes per campaign win, and open at Bay level 2/3/4.
   - **Preview vs pay:** `finishExpedition` reads `bayLevel` at finish time, while the sheet previews `towerLevel` at send time, so what is paid can differ from the preview. M13 fixes the yield at send time.
   - **Rates:** rounds pay ember, leaf, dew and stone at 1 per 2 sectors and only frost at 1 per sector (constellations.ts `dropsFor`, RM:392). Trips use the same rates.
4. **The galaxy doesn't store lands.** `GalaxyPlanet` (profile.ts:37-45) holds only the top 4 biome colours. Both "that planet's Essences" and "the postcard shows the real planet" need a save change. The renderer needs biome, land, water, life and species for each of 24 sectors.
5. **No 1.0 golden exists, for saves or for the calendar.** Save fixtures stop at v4 (`i-pre-m115.v4.json`), but 1.0 ships v6. No golden protects the Voyage weeks or Daily Planets that 1.1 must not change. Voyage stops are seeded with the week's theme at runtime (voyage.ts:108-109), and `p.voyage` doesn't store that theme (voyage.ts:58-72). Each milestone's step 0 captures the previous release's save, and from M13 its Voyage-week and Daily-date hashes, from the untouched code.
6. **M14's point order needs a decision before work starts.**
   - `grantProfileRoadPoints` fills unfinished Past Roads before the current one (starroad.ts:171), as RM §5.5 (RM:608) and RM:672 promise.
   - The launch-cohort Regular finishes Road 0 (42-56 days) before Road 1 opens (day 56), so the order matters for two other groups:
     - new installs after Road 1 opens would fill Road 0 first and give Road 1 nothing for about 50 days (B3(b) fails);
     - slow players (about 100 days a Road) would never finish Road 0 under a strict current-first rule, and a Road 0 Pass lane would stall.
   - Decision 75 shares points between the two Roads.
7. **Two 1.0 launch items aren't M13 work.**
   - `npm run ios:release` throws at HEAD for 1.0.0: its regex (ios-release.mjs:16-19) doesn't match `export const VERSION: string = version;` (tuning.ts:10).
   - LR lists the iPad compatibility pass for launch (LR:10, LR:214).
   - Both must be done before the 1.0 submission (Appendix §0). M13 only keeps regression checks for them.

  - Frost balance: 174 on day 28 (Ice Lab 5 still waiting), 4 on day 29, 180 on day 60 and 504 on day 90. No frost sink is left after Ice Lab 5 and HW L5.

### 0.3 Hard rules every package checks (both milestones)

- No gambling or random paid rewards. Items are fixed-price and can be previewed. Nothing sold adds power or speeds growth (the payer must match the no-purchase twin, decision 55).
- The parental gate (Gate v2) sits in front of every outbound action and every purchase. Shares go through `shareCanvas`, which already awaits `parentalGate('share')`. The policy lint (`tests/policy.test.ts`) must stay green.
- The kid side shows no prices. There are no energy timers and no way to pay to skip. Copy never says "come back later" and contains no urgency words (`URGENCY_WORDS`, and the `/\+\d+%/` pattern, so a match bonus is never shown as "+25%"). It also uses no energy-system words ("fuel", "energy", "recharge").
- Times show as clock times through `whenText` (dates.ts:26) or as plain dates through `untilText`. Nothing counts down.
- No notification ever comes from the Homeworld, trips included. The limit stays at most 1 per calendar day, none between 21:00 and 09:00.
- Strings go through `t()`/`tp()` in 6 languages. The `i18n`, `terms`, `glossary`, `nouns` and `policy` tests stay green, and from M14 they also read the per-Road content fragments.
- Reduce Motion: every new moment uses the M6.5 motion kit (skippable, under 2 s, a fade and a pose under Reduce Motion). Layout works at 320×568 with 44 px targets. VoiceOver has labels and a list view.
- Privacy: the label stays "Data Not Collected", and `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` keeps its pinned SHA. There are no network APIs and no new npm dependencies (`tests/privacy.test.ts:12-26` allowlist). Defaults are high-privacy: anything that stores data off the device is off until a grown-up turns it on.
- Owner-only actions: anything in App Store Connect or the Developer portal (capabilities, products, In-App Events, screenshots, submissions). We prepare drafts and files and never create those objects ourselves.
- Saves: every format change adds a fixture written in the old format, and existing fixtures are never edited (saves.test.ts:1-6).
- Sims: a change to sim _policy_ (what the bot does) lands on the previous release's code with a new baseline before any game change is measured against it. Gate drift must always trace to the game.

# Part 1: M13 Friends and trips (update 1.1)

## M13 §1 Goal and acceptance

**Goal.** Friends become the heart of the daily loop.

- **The trip.** A child picks one of their own planets for a trip. The game chooses the friend who will love it most and shows exactly what will come back (that planet's Essences, a few gems, friendship). The return time is a clock time. Every win brings the friend home 10 minutes sooner.
- **The return.** The friend comes back with a postcard of the real planet, which can be shared only through the gate.
- **Wishes** now come first from friends who live with you, asking for feats on their home lands.
- **Backup and iPad.** Progress gets an Apple-hosted iCloud backup that a grown-up turns on. The iPad gets a dormant wide layout.
- **Economy.** Trips become the main extra source of frost without out-earning play in any colour.

**Acceptance.** All of these must hold. Gates fail the build; Watches are printed and reviewed. "Bay-3 day" is the day Regular's Bay first reaches effective level 3 under the step-0 sim build order (decision 56).

**A9 inherited economy gates.**

- Idle ≤ 1.5× active every active day 1-60.
- HW L5 ≥ day 28 (Gate) and ≤ day 60 (Watch).
- All Labs 5 ≥ day 28, and Engaged ≥ 30% sooner.
- Every currency has a sink on day 60.
- Free gems ≥ 95 per active day.
- Free boosters ≤ 0.5 per win. Greenhouses ≤ 1/3.
- Payer = no-purchase twin on HW L5, Labs and Beacon, on both seeds.
- Replays ≤ 30% of Essences (Watch).
- From M12, because Road 0 is live in 1.1: Regular finishes Road 0's free lane in 42-56 days, and new Road gem sinks ≥ 0.8× free gem supply over days 0-56 (economy.sim.ts:151-160 and :265-268; PR soft, nightly hard, as today).
- Colour stranding (Watch), plus a frost and ember surplus Watch: balance on days 60 and 90, and trip frost and ember earned after the last sink of that colour is bought.

**A12 iCloud backup.**

- A complete no-op when `PPCloudBackup` is absent or false, when there is no iCloud account, or until a grown-up turns backup on: 0 plugin store calls.
- Each device writes only its own key (`pp.b.<saveId>.<deviceNonce>`), so it never overwrites another device's copy.
- `saveProfile` doesn't change the profile, so `saveProfileChecked` still passes when a backup or another save interleaves.
- It never restores without the gate, and it refuses a newer-version backup.
- Restore keeps this device's fields from the list in decision 72, merges gems from purchases the backup lacks or refuses, and clamps clocks.
- One compressed key from a 180-day career ≤ 96 KB; raw JSON ≤ 256 KB.

**Trip yield table (decision 58; starting values).**

- **Base:** `b_c` per colour comes from the trip planet's 24 sectors at the round rates: frost counts 1 per sector of its lands; ember, leaf, dew and stone count 1 per 2 sectors, rounded down (constellations.ts `dropsFor`, RM:392). There is no star bonus.
- **Tuning:** package A may tune only F₁ and F₄, inside the flat per-hour rule F₁ ≤ F₄/4 ≤ F₈/8 (unit-tested; at most 0.156 and 0.625). F₈, the caps and the gems never move.

| Hours | Bay level | Essence factor F | Cap per colour | Gems | Friendship (no match / match) |
| ----- | --------- | ---------------- | -------------- | ---- | ----------------------------- |
| 1     | 2         | 0.15             | 2              | 1    | +1 / +1                       |
| 4     | 3         | 0.60             | 6              | 2    | +1 / +2                       |
| 8     | 4         | 1.25 (fixed)     | 10             | 3    | +2 / +3                       |

`yield_c = min(cap, round(b_c × F × (match ? 1.25 : 1)))`, with zero colours dropped. A planet whose yield is all zeros is never offered.

**Match.** The friend's home land (S's `homeLandFor`: `home[0]`; Leviathan ocean; World Tree Spirit forest) covers at least 3 of the planet's 24 sectors.

**Worked example, 6 frost sectors:**

- 8 h + match: 9.4, rounded to 9 frost (A5).
- 8 h without a match: 7.5, rounded to 8.
- 4 h + match: 4.5, rounded to 5.
- 1 h + match: 1.1, rounded to 1.

RM's "8-10 frost" holds for any F₈ from 1.0 to 1.39, but F₈ is pinned at 1.25 so A5 stays exact.

**Per-hour shape.**

- Before rounding and caps, the Essence factor per hour is flat (0.150, 0.150 and 0.156), so a short trip never pays more Essence per hour than a long one.
- Gems per hour rise with length.
- Friendship per hour is highest for 1-hour trips, which is why decision 59 ties every send to a win.
- There is no rarity bonus, no stardust and no boosters.

## M13 §6 Conflicts, risks and decisions to record

**Risks:**

- **R1 Labs margin.** "All Labs 5 ≥ day 28" has 5 days of margin (Regular day 33). Two forces pull in opposite directions:
  - **Later:** the Bay's stardust (800 + 2,000 + 4,800 + 11,200 = 18,800 ✨) and its plot delay Labs, which are stardust-bound on days 8-11 and 19-25. They also push HW L5 toward its day-60 Watch.
  - **Earlier:** trip ember, frost and leaf speed the last Lab-5 purchases, which are Essence-bound on days 26-32 (Ice on frost until day 29, Sun on leaf until day 30, Magma on ember until day 33).
  - Step 0 measures the first force alone; M13 adds the second. Mitigation: decision 61.
- **R2 Frost band.** The band is broken at HEAD (19.8 vs 3-10); see decision 57.
- **R3 Friendship farm.** Friendship from idle trips risks a farm loop that conflicts with HW:408 (§4.9 rule 6) and pillar 3 (RM:82); see decisions 59 and 60.
- **R4 Preview vs pay.** The 1.0 payout code can pay something different from the preview; fixing the yield at send closes it for 1.1 trips.
- **R5 Downgrade.** 1.0 can't load a 1.1 save (`migrate` throws on v > 6), so a restore or downgrade to 1.0 is refused. That is acceptable, and the copy says "Update Comet Garden".
- **R6 Key-value store semantics.** The store is eventually consistent and last-writer-wins, and siblings may share an Apple ID. A shared key can't be protected by sequence checks, because a device with a stale copy passes them. Handled by one key per device per progress line (decision 68): no device ever writes another's key.
- **R7 iPad physics.** A tablet viewport would change flight physics (fixed-px launcher and gravity); handled by the logical frame (decision 73).
- **R8 Release script.** `ios:release` throws today for 1.0.0, so it blocks the 1.0 submission (Appendix §0), not only 1.1.
- **R9 More friends in the sim.** More friendship gems raise free gem supply. That moves M12's gem-sink gate (A9) and raises M14's Road 1 gem-sink target (decision 81).
- **R10 `UIRequiresFullScreen`.** Reported as deprecated in iPadOS 26. It doesn't matter while the app stays in iPhone compatibility mode, but re-check it before any native iPad flip.
- **R11 Japan.** M13 adds a free-gem faucet (≤ 3 per trip), and a restore copies a gem balance to a new device. That goes to counsel (decision 72).

## M14 §6 Conflicts, risks and decisions to record

**Risks:**

- **R1 Shared code paths.** Road 0 is wired into about 15 call sites (§2 14.1). Missing one leaves Road 1 looks unowned or a refund unrevoked. Mitigated by a per-Road test sweep that runs every check for both `road00` and `road01`.
- **R2 Catch-up depends on install timing.** It is keyed to `opensOn`, so a late build or a phased rollout shortens Road 0's catch-up window. Mitigated by decision 76's 28 days.
- **R3 Unprotected weeks.** Closed by M13's frozen-week and frozen-day goldens and the saved `voyage.theme`, all in place before any almanac row.
- **R4 Runway runtime.** 51.2 s for 98/14 measured, too slow for `npm test`'s 30 s, so it gets its own job.
- **R5 Permanent product IDs.** App Store Connect product IDs can't be changed; the frozen sheet and owner approval come first.
- **R6 Rising gem target.** More gems from M13 raise the sink target. Measure first, then size the singles.
- **R7 Sixth Landmark.** It collides with the 5-site circle (`sitePoint` divides by 5), so it uses a separate list and the Isle site.
- **R8 Event limits.** Apple's In-App Event limits (≤ 31 days, promotion up to 14 days early) are my understanding and must be verified. An event is shorter than a 56-day Road.
- **R9 Product name length.** "Cosmic Pass: X" leaves about 14 characters for the Road name in French and Portuguese.
- **R10 M16 precondition.** The Collector's Edition needs ≥ 3 Themes and a Past Road; Road 1 with a Theme meets both.
- **R11 Achievements.** The Game Center point cap is full, so Road 1 adds no achievements.
- **R12 App Review can't find hidden products** (guideline 2.1). Closed by listing all 3 products in Grown-ups from install and the review notes (decision 77).
- **R13 Import cycle.** If `road01.ts` imported `cosmetics.ts` while cosmetics imports `road01`, the app would throw at startup. Closed by `ThemeSpec` as data (14.1).
