## Kept deliberately

- `@capgo/native-purchases` dependency, lockfile and dependency allowlist; the owner decides removal. Native/source exclusion has a regression guard.
- `splash` on modifiers, Scene options, impact/rule functions and historical frozen variants; REACH_CAP is also left with that engine. Zero production splash does not justify discarding saved/rules-test splash=1 behavior under RULES_VERSION 1.
- `EVENT_TIERS`, EventTier and old event week/claimed/retired/value data, eventFor's legacy week mapping, retirement and atmosphere/catch-up queries: old saves and Voyage migration still read these.
- Retired building IDs and historical refunds, producer accrual and observatory/Greenhouse conversion: they preserve already-paid value.
- Saved rank, m4RankPaidThrough, rank-named stable achievement IDs and old mode/banner/frame grandfathering: genuine old save readers remain. Only rank naming/UI is retired.
- Lab maximum, ordinary building tiers, five Vault tiers and historical level-5 refund/rate caps are independent contracts. Literal Level 5 opening/copy describes that authored stage; it is not used as a load/expansion limit. Extending Homeworld still requires new requirements, plot counts and authored level content.
- Optional purchase markers stay optional, preserving the meaning of an absent legacy Pass marker. Starter/pass bits and their durable reset behavior are retained; no money-path storage redesign was attempted.
- No unconditional “extra locale keys” failure was added: retiring keys is the lead's in-progress locale work, and old compatibility dictionaries are intentionally tolerated. Missing-key coverage still passes after the merge.
