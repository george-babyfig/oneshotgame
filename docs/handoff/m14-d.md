# M14 package D: implementation and integration report

Free-supply numbers are published first in [m14-d-supply.md](m14-d-supply.md). Scenario dates are not a player-visible launch commitment. No launch date was supplied; live Road 1 remains dormant under decision 81.

D defines Road Landmark `rl_road01` / **Sky Loom**, earned decoration `decor_sky_loom` and earned title `title_sky_weaver`. Three play stages plus one delivery build (four displayed stages). The two grow stages each accept 12 Meadow/Forest/Savanna sectors OR 12 Marsh/Jungle/Highland sectors. The middle stage accepts 8 Fusions OR 8 Supernovas. Delivery is 20 leaf + 20 dew + 20 stone. No currency rewards, Road points, Pass prerequisite or automatic placement. Every stage requires a deliberate Build.

The only `roadLandmarkMail(p)` lives in `src/meta/roadLandmarks.ts`. A's Inbox integration must call it from `checkMail` and call `roadLandmarkLetter(m)` from `letterOf`. Both hooks are already present on the shared tree. `recordRoadLandmarkRound` is called by `roundSettlement`. `roadLandmarkOpen(p,id)` uses the playable catalogue and Beacon completion, persists into Past Roads, and queues after earlier Road Landmarks.
